Our Story & Heritage

From Our Farm in Mancherial to Every Telugu Dining Table

"For me, Arjun Dairy Farm is not just a business — it is our family's devotion to preserving the authentic, unpolluted taste of village food."

As millions of viewers on Sandya Mana Telugu Ammayi have witnessed through our regular daily vlogs, our cattle are nurtured with green fodder, dry grass, and mineral care without any hormone injections or artificial stimulants.

Every drop of milk is collected under strict hygienic conditions. Our ghee is prepared following the time-honored Bilona Method — where fresh whole milk is converted to curd, churned with wooden churners to extract makkhan (butter), and then gently simmered on a wood-fire flame to produce golden, aromatic grains.
