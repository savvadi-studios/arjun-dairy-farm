Why Our Dairy Tastes Unmatched

Grass-Fed & Loved Cattle

Our Desi Gir cows and Murrah buffaloes graze freely on natural green grass, dry sorghum, and fresh water. Zero hormone or oxytocin injections.

🪵

Vedic Bilona Churning

We culture whole milk into curd and churn it with bi-directional wooden churners before slow-simmering on wood fires to preserve essential nutrients.

Know What You Drink

Factory Packet Milk vs. Arjun Farm Fresh Milk

Discover why hundreds of families switch to Arjun Dairy Farm for the health and vitality of their children and elders.

❌ Factory Processed Packet Milk

  • ⚠️ Fat is stripped, skimmed, and artificially re-engineered
  • ⚠️ Homogenized at extreme pressures, breaking natural milk fat globules
  • ⚠️ Stored in cold warehouses for days before transport
  • ⚠️ Synthetic preservatives added to extend plastic shelf life
  • ⚠️ Yields negligible malai and produces watery, tasteless curd

✅ Arjun Dairy Farm (Pure Farm Milk)

  • ✨ 100% Raw, Full-Cream Whole Cow & Buffalo Milk
  • ✨ Delivered within hours of milking; natural enzymes and nutrients preserved
  • ✨ Forms a thick, aromatic layer of golden malai upon boiling
  • ✨ Sets thick, naturally sweet, probiotic-rich curd (perugu)
  • ✨ Zero preservatives, artificial thickeners, or chemicals
